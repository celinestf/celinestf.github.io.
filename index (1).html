about me

Hi, I’m Celline Stefanny, a graphic designer and illustrator specializing in detailed flat design with a refined pastel aesthetic. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Communication Studies, which shapes my approach to visual communication: every design is built to convey its message clearly to its intended audience.

I create and publish original stock illustrations on Adobe Stock, developing cohesive collections that meet professional marketplace standards.

My work covers stock illustration, social media design, and visual branding for lifestyle and small business brands. I focus on consistency, attention to detail, and clean, purposeful composition.

project 1

Social Media Design – Anne & Zane

Instagram feed · e-commerce visuals · live banners

Anne & Zane is a local SME brand that produces children’s activity toys and flashcards. As the graphic designer, I created Instagram feed layouts and e-commerce visuals with a colorful, playful approach, tailored to children aged 4–12 and their parents as the main buyers.

I also designed banners for live streaming on e-commerce platforms (Shopee and Tokopedia), since banners and titles are key visual elements that viewers see during live sessions.

View the designs on Instagram: @anneandzane
